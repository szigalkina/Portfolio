Blijdorp — the neighbourhood app for mothers

Meet Moms Kids Journal

An iOS app I designed and built end to end with Claude Code, now live on the App Store. It is really two apps in one. Meet Moms puts the mothers on your own streets on a warm map (by neighbourhood, never an exact address) and turns a walk or a coffee into a one-tap invite anyone free can accept. Kids Journal lets you talk for ten seconds in any language; each note becomes a dated album page you can share with family by link and export as a print-ready book. Built on React Native and Supabase: realtime, push notifications, and Apple's full privacy and safety review.

Energy Transition

Six years of energy domain expertise. Building decarbonisation, traceability, monitoring, and reporting software for energy products — many without precedent, vendor, or playbook.

Carriers

Green Power, Steam, Hydrogen & Microgrids

24/7 energy monitoring for Shell and customers across power, hydrogen, and steam — built in response to market demand and incoming regulation. RFNBO compliance for hydrogen, certificate traceability for green power, and identification frameworks for steam. Each product shipped as a combined digital and commercial offering, launched before the plants were operational and before the customers knew they needed it.

Storage

Thermal Energy Storage & Battery

The energy grid is inflexible — it can't easily absorb surplus or respond to demand spikes, and that rigidity is the bottleneck. Storage is what makes it manageable: capturing energy when the grid is full, releasing it when demand peaks. Built digital products to monitor and report on energy and the carbon footprint in battery storage and thermal energy storage. No vendor to evaluate, no prior art to follow. Worked directly with plant engineers to understand how the assets behaved, then translated that into a software solution and business model from scratch.

Optimisation

P2P · EMS · VPP

Deep domain work across Peer-to-Peer Energy Exchange, Energy Management Systems, and Virtual Power Plants. Energy only gets optimised when the actors orchestrate together — that coordination layer is where the value lands.

Regulation

EU + US Frameworks

Close monitoring of regulation and compliance — RED III, RFNBO rules, GHG standards, energy certifications (Guarantees of Origin, RECs, Granular Certifications), and Digital Product Passport — while tracking development in voluntary market standards before they became mandatory. Translated regulatory signals into product requirements before the deadlines landed.

✦

The differentiator: thermal storage. Almost no PM has touched it — physics, commercial model, and regulation that has never been productised.
