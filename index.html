Energy Transition

Six years of energy domain expertise. Building decarbonisation, traceability, monitoring, and reporting software for energy products — many without precedent, vendor, or playbook.

Carriers

Green Power, Steam, Hydrogen & Microgrids

24/7 energy monitoring for Shell and customers across power, hydrogen, and steam — built in response to market demand and incoming regulation. RFNBO compliance for hydrogen, certificate traceability for green power, and identification frameworks for steam. Each product shipped as a combined digital and commercial offering, launched before the plants were operational and before the customers knew they needed it.

Storage

Molten Salt & Battery Storage

The energy grid is inflexible — it can't easily absorb surplus or respond to demand spikes, and that rigidity is the bottleneck. Storage is what makes it manageable: capturing energy when the grid is full, releasing it when demand peaks. Built digital products to monitor and report on energy and the carbon footprint in battery storage and molten salt thermal. No vendor to evaluate, no prior art to follow. Worked directly with plant engineers to understand how the assets behaved, then translated that into a software solution and business model from scratch.

Optimisation

P2P · EMS · VPP

Deep domain work across Peer-to-Peer Energy Exchange, Energy Management Systems, and Virtual Power Plants. Energy only gets optimised when the actors orchestrate together — that coordination layer is where the value lands.

Regulation

EU + US Frameworks

Guarantees of Origin, RED III, RFNBO rules, RECs. How certification markets and trading interact.

✦

The differentiator: thermal storage. Almost no PM has touched it — physics, commercial model, and regulation that has never been productised.
